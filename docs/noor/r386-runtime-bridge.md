Receipt identity must use authenticated owner and stable client request ID, hash of normalized logical command, stored committed revision and bounded response metadata. Do not store full state in production receipts or send stale snapshots after late retries. A receipt lookup after lost acknowledgement must not execute business mutation again. Delivery transitions need explicit worker semantics, retention/erasure and privacy review before enabling.
